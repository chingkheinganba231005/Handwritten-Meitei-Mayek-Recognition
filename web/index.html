Handwritten Meitei Mayek recognition

One character at a time, from all 55 classes of the TUMMHCD dataset: digits, letters, final consonants (lonsum), vowel signs and punctuation. The network runs in your browser; nothing you draw or upload leaves your device.

Fill the canvas the way you would fill a box on a form: the size you write at counts.

Dark ink on light paper works best. The character is cropped automatically.
